About EMR

BUILT ON MERIT OFFSHORE

Nearly two decades of offshore catering and associated services, with a focus on reliability, safety and fit-for-purpose solutions.

Company About

One of the most trusted names in offshore catering.

EMR set out to be recognized as a catering service provider delivering high-quality, cost-effective services to petrochemical industries across Malaysia. Incorporated in March 2006, EMR has earned its place on merit — today standing as a reliable, cost-effective and responsive partner across offshore operators.
