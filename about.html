Our Story

A CLEAR ASPIRATION.

EMR was incorporated on March 07, 2006, with a clear aspiration to be recognized as a catering service provider for the petrochemical industries in Malaysia and the region.

The company has developed experience in offshore catering and associated services while expanding its capabilities through partnerships and diversification.
