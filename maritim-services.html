Our Services

MARITIM SERVICES.

Comprehensive catering and support solutions for vessels, crew and maritime operations with a focus on quality, safety and consistency.

01

Luxury Yacht Catering

Premium dining experiences for luxury yachts with personalized menus and world-class chefs.

We create refined dining and hospitality experiences that reflect the elegance of each vessel while meeting exacting culinary and service expectations for guests and crew.

02

Commercial Vessel Catering

Reliable catering services for cargo ships, tankers and commercial vessels with crew nutrition focus.

Our commercial vessel service is built around consistency, food safety and tailored nutrition planning to support crews during long sea voyages and demanding operational schedules.

03

Fresh Seafood Specialties

Ocean-fresh seafood preparation with sustainable sourcing and expert culinary techniques.

From premium sourcing to precise preparation, we provide seafood menus that prioritize freshness, quality and balanced nutrition for crews and guests alike.

04

Crew Management

Complete crew catering management including meal planning, preparation and dietary requirements.

We coordinate crew welfare and hospitality services with emphasis on planning, food quality, work schedules and clear communication to support healthy and efficient operations.

05

Supply Chain Management

Efficient procurement and logistics for fresh ingredients and supplies to any port worldwide.

We support smooth maritime operations through reliable sourcing, inventory planning and efficient supply coordination, helping keep vessels fully provisioned and ready for service.

06

Safety & Compliance

Full compliance with maritime safety standards and international food safety regulations.

Our process includes strong food safety controls, operational checks and adherence to marine and hospitality regulations to protect crew welfare and maintain dependable service standards.
